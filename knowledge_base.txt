CHAPTER I: OVERVIEW AND DEFINITIONS
=======================================================
OVERVIEW

The Travel Guide AI Knowledge Base is designed to help users plan trips, explore destinations, and make informed decisions regarding travel logistics such as accommodation, transportation, budgeting, and activities.

It applies to all types of travel including leisure, business, solo, and group travel.

WHAT IS A TRAVELER?

A traveler is any person who:

Plans or undertakes a journey for leisure, business, education, or personal purposes
Seeks information about destinations, logistics, or experiences
TYPES OF TRAVELERS:
Solo traveler
Family traveler
Budget traveler
Luxury traveler
Backpacker
WHAT IS A TRAVEL PLAN?

A travel plan is a structured outline of a trip that includes:

Destination(s)
Duration
Budget
Accommodation
Transportation
Activities
WHAT IS AN ITINERARY?

An itinerary is a day-wise schedule of a trip including:

Places to visit
Travel timings
Activities
Rest periods
KEY DEFINITIONS
Destination: A place where a traveler intends to visit
Accommodation: A place where a traveler stays (hotel, hostel, Airbnb, etc.)
Transportation: Means used to travel (flight, train, bus, etc.)
Activity: Any experience or task performed during travel (sightseeing, adventure, etc.)
Budget Travel: Travel focused on minimizing expenses
Luxury Travel: Travel focused on comfort and premium experiences
